# Research

* `baselines/build20/` — the frozen build 20 study (commit a5a4f98): manifest with source/result hashes,
  parameters, splits and model identity, every recorded AI answer, and a SHA-256 per symbol-day of bars.
  `python scripts/reproduce_baseline.py REALHIST_DIR` re-runs it with `BTConfig.baseline_build20()` and
  fails unless every number matches `docs/results/2026-11-06/backtest.json`.
* `ledger.json` — which market days have already influenced development (`stockpilot/research/ledger.py`).
  Every day of the build 20 study is EXAMINED: it can be used for development, never called unseen.
* `COSTS.md` — verified fees and the economics of small accounts.
* `runs/` — later research runs, each in its own dated folder. Nothing here is overwritten.
  * `runs/2026-11-06-round1/` — round 1 (holdout 2026-05-01 → 08-26).
  * `runs/2026-11-07-reset/` — strategic reset: where trading profits come from, capital and risk simulations.
  * `runs/2026-11-11-invest-pilot/` — the proposed forward, paper-only investing pilot (AI picks vs holding VOO;
    `autotrader/invest/`): `REGISTRATION.md` and an offline dry run on synthetic data with fixture decisions
    (`dry-run/`, not a result). Not approved; the forward clock has not started.
  * `runs/2026-11-07-phase5/` — phase 5, the autonomous research-and-decision prototype (`autotrader/`): a
    development replay on examined data (not evidence) and the forward ledger (`PREREGISTRATION-2026-11-07-phase5.md`).
    Closed as inconclusive on 2026-11-11 with zero model decisions (`CLOSED.md`).
  * `runs/2026-11-07-phase4/` — phase 4, event-driven feasibility audit and the earnings-day test
    (`PREREGISTRATION-2026-11-07-phase4.md`). NO-GO. Raw data not committed; see `data-manifest.json`.
    `corrected/` holds the integrity corrections (stop timing, daily fees, ML acceptance), published separately;
    the original results are unchanged, and the corrected ones are not independent evidence.
  * `runs/2026-11-07-phase3/` — phase 3, multi-day rules on Ken French academic data (holdout A 2006-01-03 →
    2020-07-24, used once; `PREREGISTRATION-2026-11-07-phase3.md`). Research tools: `requirements-research.txt`.
  * `runs/2026-11-07-phase2/` — phase 2 (holdout 2023-09-01 → 2025-08-29). `REPORT.md` covers the design and
    results; `tables.md` is regenerated by `scripts/research_report_phase2.py`.
* `PHASE5-AUTONOMOUS.md` — the phase-5 design, what it shows and what remains unproven.
* `REOPENING-CRITERIA.md` — what new data, execution, outside evidence or capital would
  justify reopening it.
* `HOLDOUT_USED.json`, `HOLDOUT_P2_USED.json`, `HOLDOUT_P3_USED.json` — the one-time-use markers for each holdout.
* The vault (2020-07-27 → 2022-10-31) is registered as reserved and has never been downloaded.

Raw market data is not committed (Alpaca's market-data terms don't allow redistribution); the
fingerprints let anyone check that a re-download is identical.
