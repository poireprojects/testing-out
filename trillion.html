How big is a trillion a month?

Bigger than anything that exists today. Here are three ways to picture it, and how it compares with the largest companies on Earth.

  • $380,000every second, day and night, for the whole month.
  • $122from every person alive, newborns included, every single month.
  • 11%of everything the world economy produces, which is about $110 trillion a year.

The path that actually exists

Every large business climbed the same rungs in the same order. Here is what each one asks of you, from the first dollar to the trillion nobody has reached.

  1. $1once

    One stranger pays you

    Before the logo, the company name or the pitch deck, put a price on the thing and ask someone you don't know to pay it. If nobody will, you've learned that in a week instead of a year.

  2. $10,000a month

    A few hundred people keep paying

    At $30 a month that's 334 customers, few enough to win one at a time by email, calls and showing up where they are. Watch who cancels: if more than about 5% leave each month, that will cap you no matter how fast you sell.

  3. $1 milliona month

    Money goes in, customers come out

    You have at least one channel where spending a dollar predictably brings back customers. A common rule: each customer should be worth about three times what it cost to win them. This is where you start hiring.

  4. $100 milliona month

    $1.2 billion a year

    Several products, several countries, and an organization that keeps working while you sleep. Most companies that reach this size took a decade or more to get here.

  5. $57 billiona month

    Walmart

    The largest company on Earth by revenue, with more than 10,000 stores and about 2.1 million employees. No business has ever been bigger.

  6. $1 trilliona month

    17.6 Walmarts

    At this size you aren't a business inside the world economy; you're a tenth of it. No website, company or government has been here, and the math above shows why.

The step that leads toward a trillion is the same one that leads to ten thousand.
